1. 爱 | ài | love
Other meanings: like
Notes: A core verb for love and strong liking. It is common with people, food, and activities.
1. 我爱妈妈，也爱爸爸。 [primary]
   Meaning: love
   Translation: I love my mom, and I love my dad too.
2. 弟弟爱吃米饭，不爱吃肉。
   Meaning: like
   Translation: My younger brother likes rice but doesn't like meat.
3. 我爱北京，也爱那里的人。
   Meaning: love
   Translation: I love Beijing, and I love the people there too.

2. 爱好 | ài hào | hobby
Notes: Usually a noun meaning a hobby or favorite pastime. Keep it concrete and personal.
1. 我的爱好是看书和听歌。 [primary]
   Meaning: hobby
   Translation: My hobbies are reading and listening to songs.
2. 姐姐的爱好是打球和跑。
   Meaning: hobby
   Translation: My older sister's hobbies are playing ball and running.
3. 我的爱好是听歌和看电影。
   Meaning: hobby
   Translation: My hobby is listening to songs and watching movies.

3. 八 | bā | eight
Notes: A basic number word. It often appears with time, counting, and quantities.
1. 我家有八个杯子。 [primary]

5. 吧 | ba | let's
Other meanings: suggestion particle
Notes: Often softens a suggestion or invitation. It is very common at the end of a sentence.
1. 我们一起去商店吧。 [primary]
   Meaning: let's
   Translation: Why don't we go to the shop together?
2. 太晚了，回家吧。
   Meaning: suggestion particle
   Translation: It's late, let's go home.
3. 我们一起去那边坐吧。
   Meaning: let's
   Translation: Let's go sit over there together.
